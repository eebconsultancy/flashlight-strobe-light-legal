Flashlight: Strobe Light

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Flashlight: Strobe Light ("the App"), provided by EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

The App provides flashlight, lighting, signaling, and related features. Most core functionality operates locally on your device.

The App also includes optional subscriptions and advertising-supported features. We use Google AdMob to display advertisements to eligible free users.

We do not require you to create an account. Microphone audio and motion information used for lighting features are processed locally on your device. However, our advertising provider may collect and process certain device, advertising, usage, and diagnostic information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect and Process

We do not operate a user account database or a developer-operated backend that collects your personal information for the App.

Certain information may nevertheless be processed by third-party services integrated into the App, particularly Google AdMob and Apple's App Store services.

Depending on your device, location, privacy settings, consent choices, and applicable laws, advertising-related information may include:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) when access is permitted.
  • IP address and approximate geographic location inferred from network information.
  • Advertising interactions, including ad impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad completion events.
  • App interactions and product interaction information relevant to advertising delivery and measurement.
  • Device information, operating system information, and technical identifiers.
  • Diagnostic information, including performance and crash-related information.
  • Privacy preferences and consent signals used to determine permitted advertising behavior.

The precise information processed depends on the advertising services used, your choices, and the applicable Google SDK configuration.

We do not independently receive your microphone recordings, motion sensor readings, or locally stored App preferences through a developer-operated server.

3. Microphone Access

The App may request microphone access when you use sound-reactive features, such as Party mode.

Microphone input is analyzed in real time to detect audio characteristics such as rhythm, bass energy, and beat-related changes.

Audio processing occurs locally on your device. The App does not record, save, upload, or transmit microphone audio to us or to advertising providers.

You can deny or revoke microphone permission through iOS Settings. Sound-reactive features may not function without microphone access.

4. Motion and Sensor Data

Certain features may use motion information from supported device sensors, including accelerometer or related motion data.

This information may support motion-reactive lighting, cycling, walking, running, and movement-related features.

Motion information used for these features is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

5. Camera Flashlight and Device Controls

The App uses supported iOS capabilities to control the device torch for flashlight, strobe, signaling, and related lighting features.

The App may temporarily adjust supported device states, such as screen brightness or the system idle timer, while certain lighting modes are active.

These functions are used to provide the requested lighting experience and are not themselves used to collect personal information for advertising.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to display advertisements to eligible users of the free version.

Advertising formats may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and support other advertising-related purposes, subject to applicable laws and your privacy choices.

Depending on your consent and privacy settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Non-personalized advertising may still involve processing information such as IP addresses, device characteristics, and advertising interactions.

Google AdMob may use device identifiers and other information for cross-app advertising measurement or tracking where permitted by applicable law and your device permissions.

For more information about Google's data practices, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and sites

7. Advertising Consent and GDPR

Where required by applicable privacy laws, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

The consent interface may allow you to accept, reject, or manage certain advertising data processing purposes and partner preferences.

Your choices may affect whether personalized advertising is available.

Where required, the App provides a Privacy Choices option in Settings so that you can review or modify your advertising privacy preferences.

Consent choices are handled through Google's consent management infrastructure and may be stored or processed as necessary to apply those choices.

Consent is not a guarantee that all technical information processing will stop. Certain processing may remain necessary for permitted advertising delivery, fraud prevention, security, or compliance purposes.

8. App Tracking Transparency

On supported iOS devices, the App may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

ATT permission controls whether the App may access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and engage in tracking activities covered by Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules.

You may allow or decline this permission. Declining ATT permission does not prevent you from using the App's available features.

When ATT permission is denied, the App must not access the IDFA or perform tracking prohibited by Apple's policies.

ATT permission and advertising consent through Google's UMP are separate controls. Granting consent through one system does not automatically grant permission through the other.

You may review applicable tracking permissions through iOS Settings.

9. Rewarded Advertisements

The App may offer optional rewarded advertisements that allow free users to temporarily access selected Premium features.

When available, watching a rewarded advertisement may unlock the selected Premium lighting mode for a 10-minute access period.

The advertising SDK processes information associated with advertisement delivery and reward completion to determine whether the reward has been earned.

Reward eligibility and temporary access are managed by the App. Rewarded advertisements are optional, and users may choose not to watch them.

Premium subscribers do not need to watch rewarded advertisements to access their included Premium features.

10. Premium Subscriptions and Purchases

The App offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions that provide access to Premium features.

Subscription options may include a weekly subscription with an introductory free trial for eligible users and a yearly subscription without a free trial.

Current prices, eligibility, billing periods, and subscription conditions are displayed in the App and by the App Store before purchase.

Purchases, payments, renewals, cancellations, and billing are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive or store your full payment card number, bank account information, or Apple Account password.

The App may process StoreKit transaction and subscription status information to determine whether Premium access is active.

This information may include transaction verification and whether a subscription is active, expired, revoked, or pending.

Premium subscribers receive an ad-free experience within the App while their Premium entitlement remains active.

You may restore eligible purchases through Restore Purchases and manage subscriptions through Apple's subscription settings.

We do not currently use a separate third-party general-purpose analytics service to track your activity within the App.

However, Google AdMob and its associated services may process advertising-related events, engagement information, and diagnostic data for advertisement delivery, reporting, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising measurement may involve technologies and frameworks provided by Apple, including supported advertising attribution mechanisms.
