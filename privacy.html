Flashlight: Strobe Light

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Flashlight: Strobe Light ("the App"), provided by EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

Flashlight: Strobe Light is designed to operate primarily on your device. We do not operate a backend service for the App, and we do not require you to create an account.

We do not use the App to track you, serve advertising, build advertising profiles, or sell your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through our own servers or backend systems because the App does not use a developer-operated backend.

The App may access certain device capabilities and sensor data when required to provide features you choose to use. This data is processed on your device as described below.

3. Microphone Access

The App may request access to your device's microphone when you use sound-reactive features, such as Party mode.

Microphone input is used to analyze audio characteristics such as rhythm, bass energy, and beat-related changes so that the light can react to nearby sound.

Audio used for this analysis is processed on your device. The App does not use our servers to upload, store, or transmit your microphone audio, and the App does not create or save audio recordings from this feature.

You can deny or revoke microphone permission through iOS Settings. Sound-reactive features may not function without microphone access.

4. Motion and Sensor Data

Certain features may use motion information available from device sensors, including accelerometer or related motion data.

This information may be used for features such as motion-reactive lighting, activity-related behavior, and detecting changes in movement for supported cycling or running features.

Motion and sensor information used by these features is processed locally on your device. We do not receive or store this sensor data on our servers.

5. Camera Flashlight and Device Controls

The App uses supported iOS device capabilities to control the device torch for flashlight, strobe, signaling, and related lighting features.

The App may also temporarily adjust supported device states, such as screen brightness or the system idle timer, while certain lighting modes are active.

These functions are used to provide the requested App experience and are not used to collect personal information.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App may offer auto-renewable subscriptions or other purchases through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

The App may receive information from Apple's StoreKit framework that is necessary to determine purchase and subscription status, restore purchases, and provide access to premium features.

Apple's handling of purchase and account information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

7. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not display third-party advertisements.

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the App, and we do not track your activity across apps or websites owned by other companies.

We do not sell or rent personal information collected through the App.

8. Accounts

Flashlight: Strobe Light does not require you to create a user account or sign in to use the App.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Because we do not operate a backend that collects the microphone or motion information described above, we do not maintain server-side copies of that information.

The App may store preferences or settings locally on your device so that your selected options can be remembered between sessions.

Data maintained by Apple in connection with App Store purchases is subject to Apple's own data retention practices.

10. Data Sharing

We do not share microphone audio, motion sensor information, or locally stored App preferences with advertisers or data brokers.

Information related to purchases may be processed by Apple as necessary to provide App Store and StoreKit services.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us outside the App by a child, please contact us so that we can review the request.

12. Your Choices

You control whether the App may access permissions such as the microphone or motion-related capabilities through the permission controls provided by iOS.

You may review or change applicable permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

13. Security

We design the App to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of information. Features described in this policy that use microphone or motion information perform their relevant processing on the device.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but limiting unnecessary data transmission reduces exposure of this information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, applicable laws, or our practices change.

When we update this policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.
